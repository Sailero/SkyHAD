\InstancePhysics{$x_i=(p_i,v_i)\in\mathcal W\times\R^3$ is a six-value kinematic state; no quaternion, inertia, wing or rotor is attached to a particle.}
\subhead{Implemented kinematic map}
For a living, noncolliding particle, $\Delta=1$ s,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}\begin{aligned}
 p_i^*&=p_i+\Delta v_i,&p_i'&=\Pi_{\mathcal W}(p_i^*),\\
 w_i&=v_i+\Delta\alpha_i,&\widetilde v_i&=\mathcal S_{35,120}(w_i;v_i).
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
For $\norm w\ge10^{-3}$,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
\mathcal S_{m,V}(w;v)=\frac{w}{\norm w}\clip(\norm w,m,V).
\end{equation}
For $\norm w<10^{-3}$, use $m v/\norm v$ when $\norm v\ge10^{-3}$ and $m e_1$ otherwise. The cancellation fallback retains heading. After speed normalization, an outward component is set to zero on each clipped axis: upper wall $v_a>0$ or lower wall $v_a<0$.

The final 3D turn map limits the angle between old and candidate velocities to $\theta_{\max}=\pi$ per step, so the default map is identity. For a smaller cap, rotate the old heading toward the candidate about $v_i\times\widetilde v_i$, preserving candidate speed. A deterministic orthogonal axis resolves the antiparallel case; near-zero products $\norm v_i\norm{\widetilde v_i}\le10^{-3}$ have angle zero.

Position uses old velocity, with no $\tfrac12\alpha\Delta^2$ term. Boundary projection can reduce the final speed below 35 m/s. Agents killed by collision do not move; agents killed by combat complete this step's motion, and their velocity is zeroed on the next inactive update.
\subhead{Interpretation}
This is a kinematic benchmark. Its action controls future velocity and thus subsequent interception geometry, rather than instantaneous translation. Flight decisions and automatic functional decisions are distinct.

 E_-&=e^{\clip(-50(\alpha-.47),-500,500)},\\

\newcommand{\QuadControl}{%
\subhead{Geometric feedback and feasible allocation}
At a stage state, clip a held velocity reference to 20 m/s, then compute
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}\begin{aligned}
 a_d&=\sat{2(v_d-v)}{6},\\
 F_d&=m(a_d+ge_3),\\
 b_{3d}&=F_d/\norm{F_d},\\
 b_{2d}&=(b_{3d}\times e_1)/\norm{b_{3d}\times e_1},\\
 R_d&=[b_{2d}\times b_{3d},b_{2d},b_{3d}],\\
 e_R&=\tfrac12(R_d^TR-R^TR_d)^\vee,\\
 M_d&=-8.81e_R-2.54\omega+\omega\times J\omega,\\
 f_d&=F_d^TR e_3.
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
Fixed world-east heading avoids hidden yaw memory. The $6$ m/s$^2$ bound is below $g$, so $F_d$ and its heading construction are nonsingular. This uses attitude PD and gyro compensation, without the source paper's trajectory derivative or desired angular-rate feedforward.

To preserve feasible collective thrust, set $\bar f=\clip(f_d,0,4f_{\max})/4$ and $d=A^{-1}(0,M_d)^T$. Define
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_+&=\min_{d_j>0}(f_{\max}-\bar f)/d_j,\\
\kappa_-&=\min_{d_j<0}(-\bar f)/d_j,\qquad
\kappa=\min(1,\kappa_+,\kappa_-).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
 u_j=\clip\left(\frac{\bar f+\kappa d_j}{f_{\max}},0,1\right).
\end{equation}
Empty minima are ignored. Uniform differential scaling sacrifices moment while retaining the clipped collective; independent rotor clipping is not assumed to preserve it.

\CommonTitle{Common transition and physical state}{Shared sets, rigid dynamics, integration, collisions and automatic role rules}
\label{common:transition}

\subhead{Sets, symbols and complete state}
Red defenders $\mathcal R$ protect stationary assets $\mathcal P$ against Blue $\mathcal B$. Set $\mathcal U=\mathcal R\cup\mathcal B$, $\mathcal E=\mathcal U\cup\mathcal P$, $N=|\mathcal U|$, $K=|\mathcal P|$, $M=N+K$. Role $r_i\in\{A,S,D\}$ (Attack, Scout, Disturb), side $c_i\in\{R,B\}$ and roster identity $\mathrm{id}_i$ are episode-fixed. Set subscripts select roles.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:fullstate}
\begin{aligned}
s_t=\big(&\{x_i,h_i,r_i,c_i,\mathrm{id}_i\}_{i\in\mathcal U},\\
 &\{p_k,h_k,D_k\}_{k\in\mathcal P},t,\zeta_t\big).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Particle $x_i=(p_i,v_i)\in\mathcal W\times\R^3$; rigid $x_i=(p_i,v_i,q_i,\omega_i)\in\R^{13}$, $\|q_i\|=1$. Participation is $\ell_i=\ind[h_i>0]$. Reset UAV health is 1, target health $h_k^0=2$ by default; $v_k=0$, $D_k=0$ initially. $D_k$ accumulates raw asset damage.

\subhead{Six-degree-of-freedom dynamics}
Use world ENU and body FLU axes. The scalar-first unit quaternion $q=(q_w,q_x,q_y,q_z)\sim-q$ maps body to world through $R(q)\in SO(3)$. The 13 stored values represent a six-DOF rigid body with a 12-dimensional state: six configuration and six velocity coordinates.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:newton-euler}\begin{aligned}
 \dot p&=v,& \dot v&=R(q)F_B/m-ge_3,\\
 \dot q&=\tfrac12q\otimes(0,\omega),&
 \dot\omega&=J^{-1}(M_B-\omega\times J\omega).
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
Here $g=9.81$ m/s$^2$, $\omega$ is body angular velocity (rad/s), and $F_B$ excludes gravity; wind, sensor noise and actuator lag are absent.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:rotation}
 \begin{aligned}R(q)&=I+2q_w[q_v]_\times+2[q_v]_\times^2,\\q_v&=(q_x,q_y,q_z).\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Here $[z]_\times y=z\times y$.
\subhead{Numerical transition and boundary}
Write $f(x,u)$ for the above derivative, evaluated with normalized $q$. An external step is $\Delta=1$ s, $n=\lceil\Delta/.01\rceil=100$, $h=\Delta/n$. With stage feedback $U$,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:rk4}\begin{aligned}
 k_1&=f(x,U(x)),\\
 k_2&=f(x+hk_1/2,U(x+hk_1/2)),\\
 k_3&=f(x+hk_2/2,U(x+hk_2/2)),\\
 k_4&=f(x+hk_3,U(x+hk_3)),\\
 x^+&=x+h(k_1+2k_2+2k_3+k_4)/6,\\
 q^+&\leftarrow q^+/\norm{q^+}.
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
Repeat $n$ times with normalized stage quaternions. Hold actuators or intent references; recompute intent feedback at each stage. Controllers have no hidden integrators.

Clip endpoint $p'_a=\clip(p^*_a,b_a^-,b_a^+)$. At $p^*_a>b_a^+$ set $v'_a=\min(v_a^*,0)$; at $p^*_a<b_a^-$ set $v'_a=\max(v_a^*,0)$; otherwise retain $v_a^*$. Clip collision-path substep positions too. Controller reference limits do not clamp physical airspeed or attitude.
\subhead{Collision and automatic interaction}
Predict living UAV paths: particles use $p_i+\tau\Delta v_i$, rigid models use synchronized RK4 endpoints clipped to $\mathcal W$. On each matched segment, let $r=p_i-p_j$, $d=(q_i-p_i)-(q_j-p_j)$:
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:collision}
\begin{aligned}\tau_*&=\begin{cases}0&\norm d^2\le10^{-12},\\\clip(-r^Td/\norm d^2,0,1)&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}\\d_*&=\norm{r+\tau_*d}.\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Collision sets both UAV HP to zero when $\min d_*\le d_c$; targets are excluded. Pairs use $i$-major, $j<i$ order. Dead outer agents are skipped at entry and dead inner agents are skipped. An outer agent killed during its inner sweep continues that sweep.

After collision, surviving Attack agents automatically fire at step-start positions:
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:automatic-fire}
I_i=\begin{cases}
\ind[\min_{k\in\mathcal P:h_k>0}\norm{p_i-p_k}<r_f],&i\in\mathcal B,\\
\ind[\min_{j\in\mathcal B_A:h_j>0}\norm{p_i-p_j}<r_f],&i\in\mathcal R.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
An empty minimum is $+\infty$; dead sources have $I_i=0$. $A$ denotes Attack. Fire is a separate rule, not a navigation action. With unit attack intensity,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:damage-kernel}
\eta(d)=\begin{cases}1&d<r_0,\\(r_1-d)/(r_1-r_0)&r_0\le d<r_1,\\0&d\ge r_1,\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:interaction-loss}
\begin{aligned}
L_k&=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b>0}I_b\eta(\norm{p_b-p_k}),\\
L_i^{\rm atk}&=\sum_{j\in\mathcal U_A:c_j\ne c_i,h_j>0}I_j\eta(\norm{p_j-p_i}).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
A source can affect multiple enemies, excluding friendly explosions. Sum losses on one post-collision, pre-motion snapshot before clipping HP.
\subhead{Disturb and Scout}
For source $j$, $d_{ji}=\norm{p_i-p_j}$ and $\theta_{ji}=\angle(v_j,p_i-p_j)$; angle is zero if either norm is below $10^{-3}$. Let
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:disturb-sector}
\begin{aligned}
G_{ji}&=\ind[10^{-3}<d_{ji}<600\lambda,\theta_{ji}<\pi/6]\\
 &\quad\times e^{-(d_{ji}/\lambda)^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi},\\
S_c(j)&=\sum_{i\in\mathcal U:c_i=c,h_i>0}G_{ji}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Living Red Disturb uses $J_j=\ind[S_R(j)<S_B(j)]$, Blue uses $J_j=\ind[S_R(j)\ge S_B(j)]$. Dead sources contribute zero.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:health-loss}
\begin{aligned}
L_i^{\rm dis}&=.1\sum_{j\in\mathcal U_D:h_j>0}J_jG_{ji},\\
\widetilde h_i&=\max(0,h_i-L_i^{\rm atk}-L_i^{\rm dis}).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Set $h_i'=0$ if $r_i=A,I_i=1$, otherwise $h_i'=\widetilde h_i$. Disturb damages either side, not assets. Scouts use $d<2000\lambda$, $\theta<\pi/3$; this sector does not restrict native actor rows. Collision victims do not move. Combat victims complete their motion; inactive velocity is zeroed next step.
The native order is collision, role decision, losses and source consumption, motion, asset/task update, cycle increment, then reward, completion/truncation and observation. Snapshot pre-step participation; retain commands, flags and raw damage for replay.

\subhead{Observation and information structure}
For each living entity $j\ne i$, the actor row is
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:observation}
\begin{aligned}
e_{ij}=\big[&(p_j-p_i)/L,(v_j-v_i)/(2V_{\max}),\\
 &h_j,1,\ind_{c_j=R},\ind_{c_j=B},\ind_{j\in\mathcal P}\big],\\
L&=\sqrt{\sum_a(b_a^+-b_a^-)^2}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Here $V_{\max}$ is the preset maximum speed, not the reference speed. Rows have 11 values, in fixed roster order: Red Scout/Disturb/Attack, Blue Scout/Disturb/Attack, then targets, excluding self. Dead rows are zero. Every living entity is included; Attack, Scout and Disturb share this packing. Side flags do not encode role. Own health is absent from the self block.

Interface-specific self blocks and dimensions are stated in each theme. With deterministic encoding, $Z_i(o\mid s)=\delta_{\operatorname{encode}_i(s)}(o)$. Other UAV attitudes/rates, opponent memory and time are omitted; hence local information is partial despite noiseless sensing.

Privileged $g(s)=\texttt{state()}$ packs 11 values per entity,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:critic-packing}
[(p-b^-)/(b^+-b^-),v/(2V_{\max}),h,\ell,R,B,P],
\end{equation}
with dead blocks zero. Rigid models append 13 raw state values per UAV (dead zero), then $[t,\sum_kD_k,\ind_{\rm Damage}]$: size $11M+13N+3$. Particle size is $11M$, omitting time and damage. $g$ is a CTDE feature, not the complete Markov state.
\subhead{Fixed-opponent defender Dec-POMDP}
For specified frozen external navigation policy $\pi_B$, including its memory transition, define
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:defender-dec-pomdp}\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M_R=\big(&\mathcal R,\mathcal S,\{\mathcal A_i\},T^{\pi_B},\{\mathcal O_i\},Z,R,\gamma,\rho_0,H\big),\\
T^{\pi_B}(s'\mid s,a_R)&=\int\delta_{F_C(s,a_R,a_B)}(s')\pi_B(da_B\mid o_B,\zeta_t).
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
$F_C$ includes the ordered events, model-specific controlled motion and any memory update. Native execution accepts both teams' navigation actions. Automatic fire is an environment rule and does not supply Blue navigation. A rule-based or learned frozen Blue policy is an explicit experiment choice; with both teams learning the system is a partially observable stochastic game (POSG). The reset law $\rho_0$ is defined by the model physics page.

\subhead{Configuration and replay state}
Configuration $C$ fixes roster, bounds, controls and task. $\zeta_t$ contains persistent assignments and opponent memory, if used. Reactive controllers have no hidden integrators. Last command, functional bits, pre-step positions and step damage are overwritten transition records; retain them for exact reward/event replay. RNG state is required for exact replay, not actor input.
\subhead{Interpretation of partial information}
Actor inputs omit other UAV attitudes/rates, policy memory and time. Fixed roster positions and side flags do not encode role directly. Full entity inclusion therefore does not guarantee Markov observations. A recurrent policy's local history can retain its own inferred information; that policy memory also affects exact centralized value targets. Privileged $g(s)$ is an encoding for CTDE, not a complete simulation snapshot or proof of the Markov property.

\subhead{Survival payoff and completion}
Assets obey $h_k'=\max(0,h_k-L_k)$, $D_k'=D_k+L_k$.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:survival-outcome}
z(s')=\begin{cases}-1&\exists k:h_k'<10^{-3},\\
+1&\forall b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b'<10^{-3},\\0&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
Target loss has priority. Natural completion is $d=\ind[z\ne0]$; default Red reward $R_t=10z$, Blue $-R_t$. Red elimination alone does not end the world. Individual $h_i'\le0$ retires a Survival actor after its final transition. Horizon $H$ truncates without a win bonus; use global bootstrap $b_t=1-d$, including individual death and truncation. Actor action-loss masks use pre-step participation. Explicit reward weights replace the default payoff as defined on page~\pageref{common:learning}.
\subhead{Damage payoff and completion}
Assets keep finite reset health $h_k'=h_k^0$. Count raw contributions before HP clipping, including simultaneous overkill:
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
D_k'=D_k+L_k,\quad d_t=\sum_kL_k,\quad R_t=-d_t.
\end{equation}
Blue reward is $d_t$; there is no team-size, asset-count or HP normalization and no terminal Survival bonus.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
d=\ind[\forall b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b'\le0].
\end{equation}
All agent slots remain until global completion/truncation. Dead slots have zero observations, ignored actions and still receive full team $R_t$, including later damage after Red extinction. Do not sum duplicate teammate rewards. Undiscounted returns are $(-\sum_kD_k,+\sum_kD_k)$. $H$ only truncates; global bootstrap $b_t=1-d$ remains one at truncation. Actor action-loss masks use pre-step participation.
\subhead{Three distinct masks}
Entity validity masks identify living packed rows. Pre-step participation masks decide which actor actions contribute to the action loss, including a final lethal action. The global bootstrap mask suppresses successor value only at natural world completion. Actor retirement, entity death, sample truncation and global completion are separate events. When native completion coincides with $H$, natural completion takes priority.

\CommonTitle{Common learning objective and implementation index}{Decentralized histories, centralized values, grouping and primary sources}
\label{common:learning}

\subhead{MARL / CTDE objective and value history}
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
\begin{aligned}
\tau_i&=(o_{i,0},a_{i,0},\ldots,o_{i,t}),\\
\pi_R(a_R\mid\tau_R)&=\prod_{i\in\mathcal R}\pi_i(a_i\mid\tau_i),\\
\pi_R^*&\in\arg\max_{\pi_R}\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^tR_t\right].
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Expectation is over $\rho_0,T^{\pi_B},\pi_R$. Here $T$ is natural completion (possibly infinite); $H$ limits sampled rollouts. A truncated return is partial, so its successor value is retained. For $\gamma=1$, the native task is terminal Survival payoff or negative total asset damage; $\gamma<1$ selects a discounted task. Actors use local histories only. Let $\xi_t$ augment $g(s_t)$ with task state and all relevant policy history/memory, including recurrent Red actor memory and frozen Blue memory. Exact value targets condition on $\xi_t$; $g(s)$ alone need not suffice:
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}
y_t=R_t+\gamma b_tQ^-(\xi_{t+1},a_R').
\end{equation}
Here $Q^-$ is a target critic and successor actions follow the target actor policies. Use global completion, not individual death. A recurrent critic or explicit memory/state augmentation supplies the required conditioning; privileged packed features do not automatically define a Markov critic state. This defines an objective; it does not prescribe an implemented optimizer.
\subhead{Optional grouping / SMDP extension}
Grouping adds assignments, reserves, decision clocks and policy memory to state, and currently executes acceleration commands. It does not add native themes. Macro duration $d$ ends at a command opportunity, casualty or completion. With no living Red, the call continues Blue scheduling to completion/horizon and records \texttt{no\_red\_continuation=True}.
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}\bar R_t=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\gamma^jR_{t+j},\qquad y_t=\bar R_t+\gamma^d b_tV(\xi_{t+d}).\end{equation}
Current grouping Damage returns the \emph{undiscounted} sum, directly matching $\gamma=1$. A $\gamma^d$ bootstrap alone does not discount within-macro rewards. Grouping Survival uses success 0/1 and a configured horizon result (\texttt{red\_win}, \texttt{draw}, \texttt{blue\_win}); native Survival instead uses $\pm10$ and sampling truncation. Grouping Damage retains sampling truncation.

Built-in fixed Blue opponents are reactive, balanced and concentrated with a rush executor. Balanced uses target quotas; concentrated selects minimum mean-travel target. Reactive target probability is proportional to $e^{-2\,\mathrm{coverage}-.25\,\mathrm{travel}}$, with coverage $\sum_{i\in\mathcal R:h_i>0}h_i e^{-d_{ik}/(850\lambda)}$ and travel equal to mean Blue distance divided by $5000\lambda$. Native seedless resets advance the local RNG; grouping seedless reset reproduces its saved seeded initialization.

\subhead{Optional native Survival rewards}
Each formulation uses its native default payoff. An explicit four-vector $w$ replaces Survival scalar reward by $w^T\ell_i$, where $\ell_i=(r_i^{hit},r_i^{target},r_i^{enemy},10\epsilon_i z)$, where $\epsilon_i=+1$ for Red and $-1$ for Blue. All geometry below is post-step. For a living Attack agent,
\begin{equation}\stepcounter{eqanchor}\label{eq:auto-\arabic{eqanchor}}\begin{aligned}
r_i^{hit}&=\sum_{j\in\mathcal U:c_j\ne c_i,h_j>0}\max(0,1-d_{ij}/r_1),\\
r_i^{target}&=-\min_{k:h_k>0}d_{ik}/L,\\
r_i^{enemy}&=\begin{cases}-\min_{j\in\mathcal B:h_j>0}d_{ij}/L&i\in\mathcal R,\\
\min_{j\in\mathcal R:h_j>0}d_{ij}/L-1&i\in\mathcal B.\end{cases}
\end{aligned}\end{equation}
An empty comparison set gives zero, including the Blue enemy slot; dead agents have zero first three slots. ``Hit'' is a geometric proxy, not actual fire damage. Scout/Disturb keep their role scalar in slot 1 and zero slots 2--3. Let $\delta_i$ be nearest XY-wall distance and $B_i=.05e^{-\delta_i/\lambda}\ind[\delta_i<100\lambda]$. Scout slot 1 is $c_s|\mathrm{sector}_i|-B_i$; Disturb slot 1 is $\ind[J_i,h_i>0](.1c_d\sum_{j\in\mathcal U}\mathrm{sign}_{ij}G_{ij}-B_i)$, with enemy sign $+1$, friendly sign $-1$. Defaults $c_s=c_d=0$. The episode slot already contains the team payoff; adding it again doubles the terminal reward. Damage rejects these weights.
