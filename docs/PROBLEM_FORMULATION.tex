\subhead{A. Scenario and complete state}
Red defenders $\mathcal R$ protect stationary assets $\mathcal P$ against Blue $\mathcal B$. Set $\mathcal U=\mathcal R\cup\mathcal B$, $\mathcal E=\mathcal U\cup\mathcal P$, $N=|\mathcal U|$, $K=|\mathcal P|$, $M=N+K$. Episode-fixed roles are Attack, Scout or Disturb.
\[
\begin{aligned}
s_t=\big(&\{x_i,h_i,r_i,c_i,\mathrm{id}_i\}_{i\in\mathcal U},\\
 &\{p_k,h_k,D_k\}_{k\in\mathcal P},t,\zeta_t\big).
\end{aligned}
\]
#1 Participation is $\ell_i=\ind[h_i>0]$. Reset UAV health is 1, target health $h_k^0=2$ by default; $v_k=0$, $D_k=0$ initially. $D_k$ accumulates raw asset damage.

Configuration $C$ fixes roster, bounds, controls and task. $\zeta_t$ contains persistent assignments and opponent memory, if used. Reactive controllers have no hidden integrators. Last command, functional bits, pre-step positions and step damage are overwritten transition records; retain them for exact reward/event replay. RNG state is required for exact replay, not actor input.
}
\newcommand{\RigidEquations}{%
\subhead{B. Six-degree-of-freedom dynamics}
World coordinates are east-north-up (ENU); body coordinates are forward-left-up (FLU). The scalar-first quaternion $q=(q_w,q_x,q_y,q_z)$ maps body to world through $R(q)\in SO(3)$, with $q\in S^3$, $\norm q=1$ and $q\sim-q$. Thus 13 stored values represent 12 physical degrees of freedom.
\begin{align*}
 \dot p&=v,& \dot v&=R(q)F_B/m-ge_3,\\
 \dot q&=\tfrac12q\otimes(0,\omega),&
 \dot\omega&=J^{-1}(M_B-\omega\times J\omega).
\end{align*}
Here $g=9.81\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}$, $\omega\in\R^3$ is the body angular velocity in rad/s; $F_B$ excludes gravity. No wind, sensor noise or actuator lag is present.
\[
 R(q)=I+2q_w[q_v]_\times+2[q_v]_\times^2,
 \qquad q_v=(q_x,q_y,q_z).
\]
Here $[z]_\times y=z\times y$.
}
\newcommand{\RKBlock}{%
\subhead{Numerical transition and boundary}
Write $f(x,u)$ for the above derivative, evaluated with normalized $q$. An external step is $\Delta=1$ s, $n=\lceil\Delta/.01\rceil=100$, $h=\Delta/n$. With stage feedback $U$,
\begin{align*}
 k_1&=f(x,U(x)),\\
 k_2&=f(x+hk_1/2,U(x+hk_1/2)),\\
 k_3&=f(x+hk_2/2,U(x+hk_2/2)),\\
 k_4&=f(x+hk_3,U(x+hk_3)),\\
 x^+&=x+h(k_1+2k_2+2k_3+k_4)/6,\\
 q^+&\leftarrow q^+/\norm{q^+}.
\end{align*}
Repeat $n$ times. A raw actuator is held constant; an intent is held but feedback is recomputed at every RK4 stage. Controllers have no hidden integrators.

For a candidate endpoint $p^*$, set $p'_a=\clip(p^*_a,b_a^-,b_a^+)$. If $p^*_a>b_a^+$, $v'_a=\min(v_a^*,0)$; if $p^*_a<b_a^-$, $v'_a=\max(v_a^*,0)$; otherwise $v'_a=v_a^*$. Collision paths use clipped substep positions. Boundaries are task constraints; physical airspeed and attitude are not clamped to controller reference limits.
}

\subhead{B. Implemented kinematic map}
For a living, noncolliding particle, $\Delta=1$ s,
\begin{align*}
 p_i^*&=p_i+\Delta v_i,&p_i'&=\Pi_{\mathcal W}(p_i^*),\\
 w_i&=v_i+\Delta\alpha_i,&\widetilde v_i&=\mathcal S_{35,120}(w_i;v_i).
\end{align*}
For $\norm w\ge10^{-3}$,
\[
\mathcal S_{m,V}(w;v)=\frac{w}{\norm w}\clip(\norm w,m,V).
\]
For $\norm w<10^{-3}$, use $m v/\norm v$ when $\norm v\ge10^{-3}$ and $m e_1$ otherwise. The cancellation fallback retains heading. After speed normalization, an outward component is set to zero on each clipped axis: upper wall $v_a>0$ or lower wall $v_a<0$.

The final 3D turn map limits the angle between old and candidate velocities to $\theta_{\max}=\pi$ per step, so the default map is identity. For a smaller cap, rotate the old heading toward the candidate about $v_i\times\widetilde v_i$, preserving candidate speed. A deterministic orthogonal axis resolves the antiparallel case; near-zero products $\norm v_i\norm{\widetilde v_i}\le10^{-3}$ have angle zero.

Position uses old velocity, with no $\tfrac12\alpha\Delta^2$ term. Boundary projection can reduce the final speed below 35 m/s. Agents killed by collision do not move; agents killed by combat complete this step's motion, and their velocity is zeroed on the next inactive update.
\subhead{Interpretation}
This is a kinematic benchmark. Its action controls future velocity and thus subsequent interception geometry, rather than instantaneous translation. Flight decisions and automatic functional decisions are distinct.

 E_+&=e^{\clip(50(\alpha+.47),-500,500)},\\
 \sigma&=(1+E_-+E_+)/[(1+E_-)(1+E_+)],\\

 &\quad+2\sigma\operatorname{sign}(\alpha)\sin^2\alpha\cos\alpha

\end{align*}
\[
 F_B=C_f\left\{\bar QS\begin{bmatrix}

\subhead{Motor, propeller and equilibrium}
The physical constants are $m=11$ kg, $S=.55$ m$^2$, $b=2.8956$ m, $c=.18994$ m, $\rho=1.2682$ kg/m$^3$,
\[
J=C_f\begin{bmatrix}.8244&0&-.1204\\0&1.135&0\\-.1204&0&1.759\end{bmatrix}C_f^T.
\]
With $D_p=.508$, $R_m=.042$, $I_0=1.5$, $V_m=44.4$, $k_m=60/(145\,2\pi)$, the steady motor root is
\begin{align*}
 A&=\rho D_p^5 C_{Q0}/(4\pi^2),\\
 B&=\rho D_p^4 C_{Q1}V/(2\pi)+k_m^2/R_m,\\
 C&=\rho D_p^3 C_{Q2}V^2-k_mV_m\tau/R_m+k_m I_0,\\
 \Omega_p&=\max\{0,(-B+\sqrt{\max(0,B^2-4AC)})/(2A)\}.
\end{align*}
Set $z=(D_p^4\Omega_p^2/(4\pi^2),D_p^3V\Omega_p/(2\pi),D_p^2V^2)$,
\begin{align*}
T_p&=\rho(.09357,-.06044,-.1079)z,\\
Q_p&=\rho D_p(.005230,.004970,-.01664)z.
\end{align*}
Reference trim $(x^*(V_d),u^*(V_d))$ solves all six equations $\dot v=0$, $\dot\omega=0$, including propeller reaction torque. Unknowns are pitch, heading, elevator, aileron, rudder and throttle, at velocity $(V_d,0,0)$. The reset uses the 30 m/s level trim rotated to the initial heading. \newcommand{\QuadWrench}{%
\subhead{C. Four-rotor wrench and parameters}
The rotor order is front, right, rear, left, and normalized thrust fractions are $u_j\in[0,1]$. With $m=4.34$ kg, $J=\diag(.0820,.0845,.1377)$ kg\,m$^2$, arm $l=.315$ m and yaw ratio $c_\tau=.008004$ m [2],
\[

\]
\[
\begin{bmatrix}f\\M_x\\M_y\\M_z\end{bmatrix}=
A\begin{bmatrix}f_1\\f_2\\f_3\\f_4\end{bmatrix},\qquad\]\[A=\begin{bmatrix}1&1&1&1\\0&-l&0&l\\-l&0&l&0\\c_\tau&-c_\tau&c_\tau&-c_\tau\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The rigid-body force is $F_B=(0,0,f)$ and moment is $(M_x,M_y,M_z)$. Ideal level hover has $u_j=.4$. The maximum thrust-to-weight ratio 2.5 is a simulation assumption. No aerodynamic/rotor drag, gyroscopic rotor torque or motor time constant is included [3].

\newcommand{\QuadControl}{%
\subhead{D. Geometric feedback and feasible allocation}
At a stage state, clip a held velocity reference to 20 m/s, then compute
\begin{align*}
 a_d&=\sat{2(v_d-v)}{6},&F_d&=m(a_d+ge_3),\\
 b_{3d}&=F_d/\norm{F_d},\\
 b_{2d}&=(b_{3d}\times e_1)/\norm{b_{3d}\times e_1},\\
 R_d&=[b_{2d}\times b_{3d},b_{2d},b_{3d}],\\
 e_R&=\tfrac12(R_d^TR-R^TR_d)^\vee,\\
 M_d&=-8.81e_R-2.54\omega+\omega\times J\omega,\\
 f_d&=F_d^TR e_3.
\end{align*}
Fixed world-east heading avoids hidden yaw memory. The $6$ m/s$^2$ bound is below $g$, so $F_d$ and its heading construction are nonsingular. This uses attitude PD and gyro compensation, without the source paper's trajectory derivative or desired angular-rate feedforward.

To preserve feasible collective thrust, set $\bar f=\clip(f_d,0,4f_{\max})/4$ and $d=A^{-1}(0,M_d)^T$. Define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\kappa_+&=\min_{d_j>0}(f_{\max}-\bar f)/d_j,\\
\kappa_-&=\min_{d_j<0}(-\bar f)/d_j,\qquad
\kappa=\min(1,\kappa_+,\kappa_-).
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
 u_j=\clip\left(\frac{\bar f+\kappa d_j}{f_{\max}},0,1\right).
\]
Empty minima are ignored. Uniform differential scaling sacrifices moment while retaining the clipped collective; independent rotor clipping is not assumed to preserve it.
}

\subhead{E. Collision and automatic interaction}
Predict living UAV paths: particles use $p_i+\tau\Delta v_i$, rigid models use synchronized RK4 endpoints clipped to $\mathcal W$. On each matched segment, let $r=p_i-p_j$, $d=(q_i-p_i)-(q_j-p_j)$:
\[
\tau_*=\begin{cases}0&\norm d^2\le10^{-12},\\
\clip(-r^Td/\norm d^2,0,1)&\text{otherwise},\end{cases}
\quad d_* =\norm{r+\tau_*d}.
\]
Collision sets both UAV HP to zero when $\min d_*\le d_c$; targets are excluded. Pairs use $i$-major, $j<i$ order. Dead outer agents are skipped at entry and dead inner agents are skipped. An outer agent killed during its inner sweep continues that sweep.

After collision, surviving Attack agents automatically fire at step-start positions:
\[
I_i=\begin{cases}
\ind[\min_{k\in\mathcal P:h_k>0}\norm{p_i-p_k}<r_f],&i\in\mathcal B,\\
\ind[\min_{j\in\mathcal B_A:h_j>0}\norm{p_i-p_j}<r_f],&i\in\mathcal R.
\end{cases}
\]
An empty minimum is $+\infty$; dead sources have $I_i=0$. $A$ denotes Attack. Fire is a separate rule, not a navigation action. With unit attack intensity,
\[
\eta(d)=\begin{cases}1&d<r_0,\\(r_1-d)/(r_1-r_0)&r_0\le d<r_1,\\0&d\ge r_1,\end{cases}
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
L_k&=\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b>0}I_b\eta(\norm{p_b-p_k}),\\
L_i^{\rm atk}&=\sum_{j\in\mathcal U_A:c_j\ne c_i,h_j>0}I_j\eta(\norm{p_j-p_i}).
\end{aligned}
\]
One source can affect multiple enemies; friendly explosions are excluded. All losses use a common post-collision, pre-motion snapshot and add before HP clipping.
\subhead{Disturb and Scout}
For source $j$, $d_{ji}=\norm{p_i-p_j}$ and $\theta_{ji}=\angle(v_j,p_i-p_j)$; angle is zero if either norm is below $10^{-3}$. Let
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_{ji}&=\ind[10^{-3}<d_{ji}<600\lambda,\theta_{ji}<\pi/6]\\
 &\quad\times e^{-(d_{ji}/\lambda)^2/2}/\sqrt{2\pi},\\
S_c(j)&=\sum_{i\in\mathcal U:c_i=c,h_i>0}G_{ji}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Living Red Disturb uses $J_j=\ind[S_R(j)<S_B(j)]$, Blue uses $J_j=\ind[S_R(j)\ge S_B(j)]$. Dead sources contribute zero.
\[
\begin{aligned}
L_i^{\rm dis}&=.1\sum_{j\in\mathcal U_D:h_j>0}J_jG_{ji},\\
\widetilde h_i&=\max(0,h_i-L_i^{\rm atk}-L_i^{\rm dis}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Set $h_i'=0$ if $r_i=A,I_i=1$, otherwise $h_i'=\widetilde h_i$. Disturb damages either side, not assets. Scouts use $d<2000\lambda$, $\theta<\pi/3$; this sector does not restrict native actor rows. Collision victims do not move. Combat victims complete their motion; inactive velocity is zeroed next step.
}
\newcommand{\ObservationBlock}[1]{%
\subhead{F. Exact observations and CTDE information}
For each living entity $j\ne i$, the actor row is
\[
\begin{aligned}
e_{ij}=\big[&(p_j-p_i)/L,(v_j-v_i)/(2V_{\max}),\\
 &h_j,1,\ind_{c_j=R},\ind_{c_j=B},\ind_{j\in\mathcal P}\big],\\
L&=\sqrt{\sum_a(b_a^+-b_a^-)^2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
Here $V_{\max}$ is the preset maximum speed, not the reference speed. Rows have 11 values, in fixed roster order: Red Scout/Disturb/Attack, Blue Scout/Disturb/Attack, then targets, excluding self. Dead rows are zero. Every living entity is included; Attack, Scout and Disturb share this packing. Side flags do not encode role. Own health is absent from the self block.

#1 With deterministic encoding, $Z_i(o\mid s)=\delta_{\operatorname{encode}_i(s)}(o)$. Other UAV attitudes/rates, opponent memory and time are omitted; hence local information is partial despite noiseless sensing.

Privileged $g(s)=\texttt{state()}$ packs 11 values per entity,
\[
[(p-b^-)/(b^+-b^-),v/(2V_{\max}),h,\ell,R,B,P],
\]
with dead blocks zero. Rigid models append 13 raw state values per UAV (dead zero), then $[t,\sum_kD_k,\ind_{\rm Damage}]$: size $11M+13N+3$. Particle size is $11M$, omitting time and damage. $g$ is a CTDE feature, not the complete Markov state.
}
\newcommand{\SurvivalTask}{%
\subhead{G. Survival payoff and completion}
Assets obey $h_k'=\max(0,h_k-L_k)$, $D_k'=D_k+L_k$.
\[
z(s')=\begin{cases}-1&\exists k:h_k'<10^{-3},\\
+1&\forall b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b'<10^{-3},\\0&\text{otherwise}.
\end{cases}
\]
Target loss has priority. Natural completion is $d=\ind[z\ne0]$; default Red reward $R_t=10z$, Blue $-R_t$. Red elimination alone does not end the world. Individual $h_i'\le0$ retires a Survival actor after its final transition. Horizon $H$ truncates without a win bonus; use global bootstrap $b_t=1-d$, including individual death and truncation. Actor action-loss masks use pre-step participation. Explicit reward weights replace the default payoff as defined on page 1.
}
\newcommand{\DamageTask}{%
\subhead{G. Damage payoff and completion}
Assets keep finite reset health $h_k'=h_k^0$. Count raw contributions before HP clipping, including simultaneous overkill:
\[
D_k'=D_k+L_k,\quad d_t=\sum_kL_k,\quad R_t=-d_t.
\]
Blue reward is $d_t$; there is no team-size, asset-count or HP normalization and no terminal Survival bonus.
\[
d=\ind[\forall b\in\mathcal B_A:h_b'\le0].
\]
All agent slots remain until global completion/truncation. Dead slots have zero observations, ignored actions and still receive full team $R_t$, including later damage after Red extinction. Do not sum duplicate teammate rewards. Undiscounted returns are $(-\sum_kD_k,+\sum_kD_k)$. $H$ only truncates; global bootstrap $b_t=1-d$ remains one at truncation. Actor action-loss masks use pre-step participation.
}
\newcommand{\DecBlock}[1]{%
\subhead{H. Defender Dec-POMDP and MARL objective}
For a specified frozen opposing policy $\pi_B$, define
\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal M_R=\big(&\mathcal R,\mathcal S,\{\mathcal A_i\},T^{\pi_B},\\
 &\{\mathcal O_i\},Z,R,\gamma,\rho_0,H\big),\\
T^{\pi_B}(s'\mid s,a_R)&=\int\delta_{F_C(s,a_R,a_B)}(s')\\
 &\qquad\times\pi_B(da_B\mid o_B,\zeta_t).
\end{aligned}
\]
$F_C$ is the stated collision, simultaneous interaction and controlled-motion map, including any memory evolution; $\rho_0$ is the reset law. Native execution accepts both teams' actions. Freezing a rule or learned opponent is an experiment choice; with both teams learning, the model is a POSG.
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tau_i&=(o_{i,0},a_{i,0},\ldots,o_{i,t}),\\
\pi_R(a_R\mid\tau_R)&=\prod_{i\in\mathcal R}\pi_i(a_i\mid\tau_i),\\
\pi_R^*&\in\arg\max_{\pi_R}\mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^tR_t\right].
\end{aligned}
\]
Expectation is over $\rho_0,T^{\pi_B},\pi_R$. Here $T$ is natural completion (possibly infinite); $H$ limits sampled rollouts. A truncated return is partial, so its successor value is retained. #1 Actors use local histories only. CTDE critics can use $g(s)$, joint actions and recorded task/memory features:
\[
y_t=R_t+\gamma b_tQ^-(g(s'),a_R').
\]
Here $Q^-$ is a target critic and successor actions follow the target actor policies. Use global completion, not individual death. A recurrent critic or augmented state may be needed because $g$ omits history. This defines an objective; it does not prescribe an implemented optimizer.
}

\PageTitle{Sixteen defender-centric problem formulations}{SkyHAD / 3D mathematical specification / English edition}
\begin{Col}
\subhead{Purpose and scope}
This document formalizes cooperative Red asset defense against a specified opposing Blue policy, across two particle interfaces and three interfaces each for fixed-wing and quadrotor agents. Each interface has a Survival and a Damage task: exactly 16 themes, with two pages per theme. The first page of each theme defines state, action and dynamics; the second defines interactions, observations and the defender Dec-POMDP objective.

The implementation is authoritative. In particular, particle position updates use the old velocity; native actor rows include all living entities; automatic firing is separate from navigation; and a frozen opposing policy is an experimental choice, not an automatic property of the native environment. The local reference paper's Chapter II was used only as a structural guide. Its prose and alternative kinematic or health equations are not reproduced.
\subhead{Shared notation}
$\mathcal R,\mathcal B,\mathcal P$ denote Red defenders, Blue opponents and assets. $p,v,q,\omega,h,D$ are position, velocity, body-to-world quaternion, body rate, health and cumulative raw asset damage. Subscripts $A,D$ designate Attack and Disturb roles. $\lambda$ is the scene scale; $\Delta$ is one external physical step; $H$ is the caller's sampling limit. All distances use metres and time uses seconds. $\sat z L$ clips Euclidean norm, whereas $\clip$ clips components/scalars.
\subhead{Reading the formulations}
The task is cooperative within Red after freezing $\pi_B$; the full two-team interaction is a POSG when both teams learn. A rigid-body state is a constrained 13-vector, not a position-only particle. Native \texttt{state()} is a privileged feature packing and may omit policy memory or task history. Complete simulation snapshots and a mathematical Markov state contain more information.

Survival optimizes a default terminal $\pm10$ payoff and protects every asset. Damage minimizes raw cumulative asset damage while asset health stays finite and unchanged. Red team death does not create a natural world terminal event. A sampling limit does not grant a native victory.
\subhead{General grouping/SMDP extension}
Grouping assigns persistent responsibilities and currently uses acceleration executors; it does not multiply the 16 themes. Add assignments, reserve, clocks and policy memory to state. For a macro transition lasting $d$ physical steps,
\[
\bar R_t=\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}\gamma^jR_{t+j},\qquad
y_t=\bar R_t+\gamma^d b_tV(s_{t+d}).
\]
The present grouping Damage reward is the undiscounted sum, corresponding to $\gamma=1$. At Survival horizon, its configured horizon rule can assign a result, unlike native sampling truncation. Built-in opponents are reactive, balanced and concentrated, with a rush acceleration executor. A frozen external learned policy is a separate opponent specification.

\subhead{Reward customization in Survival}
Each formulation uses its native default payoff. An explicit four-vector $w$ replaces Survival scalar reward by $w^T\ell_i$, where $\ell_i=(r_i^{hit},r_i^{target},r_i^{enemy},10\epsilon_i z)$, where $\epsilon_i=+1$ for Red and $-1$ for Blue. All geometry below is post-step. For a living Attack agent,
\begin{align*}
r_i^{hit}&=\sum_{j\in\mathcal U:c_j\ne c_i,h_j>0}\max(0,1-d_{ij}/r_1),\\
r_i^{target}&=-\min_{k:h_k>0}d_{ik}/L,\\
r_i^{enemy}&=\begin{cases}-\min_{j\in\mathcal B:h_j>0}d_{ij}/L&i\in\mathcal R,\\
\min_{j\in\mathcal R:h_j>0}d_{ij}/L-1&i\in\mathcal B.\end{cases}
\end{align*}
An empty comparison set gives zero, including the Blue enemy slot; dead agents have zero first three slots. ``Hit'' is a geometric proxy, not actual fire damage. Scout/Disturb keep their role scalar in slot 1 and zero slots 2--3. Let $\delta_i$ be nearest XY-wall distance and $B_i=.05e^{-\delta_i/\lambda}\ind[\delta_i<100\lambda]$. Scout slot 1 is $c_s|\mathrm{sector}_i|-B_i$; Disturb slot 1 is $\ind[J_i,h_i>0](.1c_d\sum_{j\in\mathcal U}\mathrm{sign}_{ij}G_{ij}-B_i)$, with enemy sign $+1$, friendly sign $-1$. Defaults $c_s=c_d=0$. The episode slot already contains the team payoff; adding it again doubles the terminal reward. Damage rejects these weights.
